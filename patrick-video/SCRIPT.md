# When did Patrick Become Stupid??

**Runtime:** ~13 min (11.5 min narration + ~1.5 min show clips)
**Format:** Sinistory "file". Dated cold open, first-person investigation, sources in the description, a clearly labelled "my own read" verdict, and a closing question.

How to read this: **VO_xx** = a narration beat. For recording they're grouped into 6 parts in `VO_CHUNKS.txt`: Part 1 = VO_01-06, Part 2 = VO_07-13, Part 3 = VO_14-19, Part 4 = VO_20-23, Part 5 = VO_24-28, Part 6 = VO_29-32. **[Cxx]** = a show clip whose own audio plays. *On screen* = text or graphics I build.

---

## 0:00 May 1, 1999 (cold open)

**VO_01**: May 1st, 1999. The very first episode... And the guy who stops him... is Patrick.
*On screen:* typed date stamp `05.01.1999 // HELP WANTED // S1`, film-grain, punch-in on the rock lifting.

**[C01]** Patrick: "Where do ya think you're goin'?" ... "get, that, job!"

**VO_02**: First real scene is a pep talk. It works. Twelve years later... holds them upside down...
*On screen:* hard cut to `09.16.2011 // PET SITTER PAT`. The date counter physically rolls from 1999 to 2011.

**[C02]** "You need to work on your handwriting, SpongeBob." *(crumple SFX layered on)*

**VO_03**: ...I wanted a date... a 2023 science paper... This is the file on Patrick's brain.
*On screen:* manila folder slams onto desk. Stamp: **FILE: PATRICK STAR / SUBJECT: BRAIN**. The "brain monitor" (a running seismograph line in the corner) switches on and stays for the whole video.

## 0:55 "The dumbest guy in town"

**VO_04**: Hillenburg: "probably the dumbest guy in town."
*On screen:* quote card, typewriter reveal, source tag `Hillenburg, SpongeBob Movie DVD featurette`.

**VO_05**: Fagerbakke: "I do the big dumb stuff. That's my deal." AquaDauber.
*On screen:* Fagerbakke photo (Wikimedia Commons, Comic-Con 2009) as a paper-clipped polaroid. "AQUA" + "DAUBER" slam together.

**VO_06**: The title of this video has a problem... two different starfish.
*On screen:* split screen, S1 frame vs S7 frame, with a "?" stamped between.

## 2:05 Firmly grasp it

**VO_07** → **[C03]** "Firmly grasp it" x3 → **VO_08**
*On screen:* EXHIBIT A tag. Freeze-frame on the bandaged hand with a red circle. The brain monitor line stays high and steady.

## 3:00 An enigma

## 4:15 Push it somewhere else

**VO_12** → **[C06]** "We should take Bikini Bottom and push it somewhere else!" → **VO_13**
*On screen:* EXHIBIT C. Over B04, a map of Bikini Bottom slides sideways off the frame.

## 5:00 Dumber than a sack of diapers

**VO_14** (the dad's letter appears as a typed note on the desk) → **[C07]** → **VO_15**
*On screen:* the brain monitor *does not move* at "never a golden age". Caption: `RESULT: NO CHANGE`.

## 5:55 "A one-time thing"

**VO_16** → **[C08]** the rampage → **VO_17** → **VO_18** (Jay Lender quote, typed in full on screen, cited `Hogan's Alley #17, 2010`) → **VO_19**
*On screen:* "Flanderization" as a dictionary entry. A loop animation: the same joke card gets photocopied over and over until it fills the frame.

## 7:30 Brain coral

**VO_20** (Tibbitt's index card comes back. The red string connects it to EXHIBIT B) → **[C09]** → **VO_21** → **[C10]** "...five meters due north" / "Brain coral!" → **VO_22** → **VO_23**
*On screen:* `10.21.2005` gets a red circle and a "PRIME SUSPECT" stamp. During VO_23 the stamp is crossed out and changed to "MY READ", so the honesty beat is visual too.

## 9:30 Fifty Krabby Patties

**VO_24** → **[C11]** "Ford knee." "That's a seven, Patricia." → **VO_25** → **[C12]** "Meow, meow, meow." → **VO_26**
*On screen:* receipt printer prints `50 x KRABBY PATTY (XTRA CHEESE) ... PAID: $0.00`. The brain monitor line drops into a flatline beep at "They just send Patrick in."

## 10:55 No, this is Patrick

**VO_27** → **[C13]** "No, this is Patrick." → **VO_28**
*On screen:* phone ring SFX. `07.09.2021 // THE PATRICK STAR SHOW` premiere card. Final line is a phone hang-up click.

## 11:50 All head

**VO_29** → **VO_30** → **VO_31**
*On screen:* real starfish photos (Wikimedia Commons). Screenshots of the Stanford news release and the Nature paper title. A simple diagram I build: nerve ring + 5 radial nerves, then "HEAD" genes lighting up the center and every arm. The corner brain monitor gets relabelled "BRAIN: NOT FOUND."

## 13:00 So when did it happen?

**VO_32**: the verdict and the question.
*On screen:* the corkboard pulls back to show the whole investigation. Two dates circled: `05.01.1999 (ON PAPER)` and `10.21.2005 (MY PICK)`. End card.
