### vo_01  (cold open)
```
This little guy has lost the same fight every single week since 1999.

Twenty-seven years. Hundreds of plans. Robots. Mind control. Disguises. He once renamed an entire town after himself. And every time, he walks away with nothing. Most of the time, Mr. Krabs just flicks him out the door.

For most of my life, I thought that was the joke. Tiny evil guy wants a burger recipe. Big crab says no. Funny.

Then I rewatched one episode. Season five, episode one. In it, Mr. Krabs tells the story of how this whole rivalry started... and he's lying.

Not a little bit. Like, the whole thing.
```

### vo_02  (the setup)
```
So today, we're putting Eugene H. Krabs on trial.

Here's how this works. I show you the evidence, straight from the show. You're the jury. And by the end, you decide who the actual villain of Bikini Bottom is.

Because I don't think it's the one who lives in a bucket.
```

### vo_03  (the official story)
```
Let's start with what everybody knows.

Across the street from the Krusty Krab, there's a restaurant called the Chum Bucket. Owner, Sheldon J. Plankton. Staff... nobody. His wife is a computer named Karen, who is, and I say this with love, the smartest person in that entire show. Keep an eye on her. She matters later.

The Chum Bucket sells chum. Nobody wants chum. So Plankton figures the only way he wins is by stealing the one thing that keeps the Krusty Krab alive. The Krabby Patty secret formula.

He goes for it in his very first episode. July 1999. He climbs inside SpongeBob's head and drives him around like a forklift.

And Krabs' explanation for all this is pretty simple. Plankton's jealous. Plankton's a failure. Plankton is evil. Case closed.

Except Krabs left a couple things out.
```

### vo_04  (Friend or Foe: the backstory)
```
April 2007. An episode called Friend or Foe. SpongeBob asks Mr. Krabs how the feud started, and Krabs tells him.

These two were best friends. Since they were babies. Two outcasts. Plankton got picked on for being a nerd, and Krabs got picked on for being broke. Like, really broke.

And there's this tiny moment. Little Krabs has one penny. One. And he spends it on a balloon... for Plankton.

Remember that penny. We're coming back to it.

So the two of them save up, they go to a burger place, the guy behind the counter won't serve them properly, and they go, forget this, we'll open our own. A burger stand. At the dump. Called Plabs Burgers.

P-L, Plankton. A-B-S, Krabs. That's real. That's in the show.
```

### vo_05  (the lie)
```
Now this is where it gets good.

Krabs tells his side, and in his version, Plankton's the problem. Plankton wanted to use the restaurant to rule over all the other kids. Plankton ruined everything. Poor innocent Eugene.

Then Plankton tells his side. And, shocker, in his version, Krabs is the problem.

And then Karen speaks up. The computer. The one person in this whole mess with no reason to lie. And she tells what actually happened.

Their burgers were awful. Nobody came. Then finally, one customer shows up. Old Man Jenkins. He eats a patty... and gets poisoned. Down he goes.

And instead of, I don't know, getting help, these two start fighting over whose fault it is. They grab the recipe and rip it in half. And in the middle of the fight, a whole shelf of ingredients falls over, lands on a patty, and... that's it. That's the first Krabby Patty.

It was an accident. They made it together.

And Krabs walked out with it.
```

### vo_06  (the reframe)
```
So let's just say it out loud.

The most famous recipe in Bikini Bottom was invented by two people. One of them got a restaurant. The other one got a bucket.

When Plankton tries to steal the formula... he's kind of just trying to steal back half of his own homework.
```

### vo_07  (Krabs' rap sheet)
```
Okay. But maybe that was one bad day. People change. So let's look at Mr. Krabs' record since then. I've been keeping count.

He charges his own employees to use stuff in the building he makes them work in.

He's price gouged his own customers, more than once.

He won't even sell Plankton a Krabby Patty. Not let him steal one. Sell him one. For money. A man who loves money more than oxygen turns down actual money, because that's how scared he is of Plankton.

And my personal favorite. Season three. Krabs eats a rotten Krabby Patty so he doesn't have to throw it away. Ends up in the hospital. Gets a visit from the Flying Dutchman. Promises to change his ways... doesn't. And when the Dutchman comes back for him, Krabs sells him SpongeBob's soul instead.

For sixty-two cents.

Sixty-two. He couldn't even round it up to a dollar.
```

### vo_08  (fair is fair: Plankton's record)
```
Now. I'm not gonna sit here and pretend Plankton's a saint, because the defense would fall apart in about four seconds.

In the first movie, Plankton steals King Neptune's crown and frames Mr. Krabs for it. Krabs gets frozen solid. And while everybody's distracted, Plankton puts a mind control bucket on every single head in town and renames Bikini Bottom after himself. Planktopolis.

That's not a guy who wants his half of a recipe back. That's a guy who wants to be king.

So yeah. He's evil. He tells you he's evil, constantly, at full volume. That's kind of his whole thing.

But then you hit this one episode, and it doesn't add up.
```

### vo_09  (the episode where Plankton wins)
```
There's an episode where Plankton actually wins.

Season three. The Algae's Always Greener. Plankton uses a machine to swap his life with Mr. Krabs. And it works. He wakes up owning the Krusty Krab. The customers. The money. The formula. Everything he's ever wanted, just sitting there.

Meanwhile, Krabs is across the street in the Chum Bucket, and honestly? He's having a pretty nice time.

And Plankton can't handle it. By the end of the episode, he wants his old life back. His sad, empty bucket.

So think about that. The guy finally gets the prize... and gives it back.

Which means the formula was never really the point.
```

### vo_10  (the actual answer)
```
So what is the point?

Here's my theory. Plankton doesn't want the recipe. He wants to beat Krabs. Specifically Krabs. The one guy who knew him before he was a villain. The guy who ran a burger stand with him at a dump, and then walked off with the thing they made together.

And Krabs? Krabs needs Plankton way more than he'd ever admit. Think about how much of that man's personality is guarding one piece of paper. The safe. The alarms. The paranoia. Without Plankton across the street, the formula's just a recipe. With Plankton across the street, it's treasure.

And when the formula actually goes missing, in the second movie, Sponge Out of Water, what happens? Bikini Bottom falls apart. Almost overnight. Full apocalypse. And who does SpongeBob team up with to get it back?

Plankton.

Turns out these two only work as a pair. They always did. It was written on the sign.

Plabs.
```

### vo_11  (verdict + ending)
```
So. The verdict.

Is Plankton a villain? Yeah. Obviously. He tells you every episode.

Is Mr. Krabs a villain? ...Members of the jury, he sold his best employee's soul for sixty-two cents.

You decide. Pick a side in the comments, I genuinely want to see the split.

But I keep coming back to that penny.

Little Eugene Krabs had one penny. And he spent it on his best friend. No deal. No profit. Nothing in it for him.

And I'm pretty sure that was the last penny Mr. Krabs ever gave anybody.

Plankton's been trying to get him to give something back ever since.
```
