### 01 · Cold open
```
On July thirty-first, nineteen ninety-nine, a guy about the size of a sesame seed walked into a burger place, screamed at the owner that he went to college, and got thrown out.

He has been getting thrown out of that same restaurant ever since. Twenty-seven years. Same door. Same crab.

He's married to a computer. He has a pet amoeba named Spot. He owns a restaurant across the street, and for a while, his one regular customer was secretly being paid to eat there.
```

### 02 · The hook
```
And the crab who keeps throwing him out used to be his best friend. Like, best friend since they were babies. They grew up together, they started a business together, and they invented the Krabby Patty together. We'll get to that. It is so much dumber than you think, and somehow also really sad.

I went back and rewatched a stupid number of Plankton episodes. More than a grown adult should. And somewhere around the one where Mr. Krabs dresses up as his own daughter to psychologically wreck a man, I realized I'd been watching this show wrong for my entire life.

This rivalry is a breakup. Two guys who split up over a burger in a garbage dump, and then spent the rest of their lives finding excuses to see each other.

And one of them is the bad guy. I'll tell you which one at the end... and I don't think it's the one with the evil laugh.
```

### 03 · Who is this guy
```
Quick refresher on who we're dealing with.

Sheldon J. Plankton. Owner of the Chum Bucket, which sits directly across the street from the Krusty Krab. Which is a bold business decision. That's opening a lemonade stand in the Costco parking lot and calling it strategy.

Fun fact. Stephen Hillenburg originally wanted Plankton so small you'd need a magnifying glass to see him. The animators talked him out of it, because you can't really animate a fight with a guy nobody can see.

He was also only supposed to be in one or two episodes. Nickelodeon wanted a famous guest star to voice him, and Hillenburg pushed back. He basically said, no... it's Doug. Doug Lawrence. Mister Lawrence. The man who has voiced every single one of Plankton's failures since.
```

### 04 · The plan, every time
```
And the plan is always the same. Steal the secret Krabby Patty formula. Make Krabby Patties at the Chum Bucket. Put Mr. Krabs out of business. And then, depending on the episode, rule the world.

It never works.

Except one time. And that time went so badly that we'll need to talk about it separately.
```

### 05 · Chapter: Three liars and a computer
```
So how do two best friends end up throwing stuff at each other across a street for the rest of their lives?

The show actually answers this. Season five premiere, two thousand seven, an episode called Friend or Foe. SpongeBob asks Mr. Krabs why he hates Plankton so much, and what we get is the same story told three different times, by three different people.

A DVD reviewer back then compared it to Rashomon. The Kurosawa movie. About a SpongeBob episode. And honestly he had a point, because nobody in this story tells the truth until the computer does.
```

### 06 · Version one, version two
```
Version one. Mr. Krabs tells it.

In Krabs's version, he is a sweet, generous little boy, and Plankton is a tiny evil genius who wants to use burgers to control all the other children. Krabs tries to stop him, gets tossed into a pile of garbage for his trouble, and the friendship is over. Very noble. Krabs is the hero. Weird how that worked out.

Version two. Plankton tells it.

It's the same story. Same scenes, same dump, same everything... except now Krabs is the evil one and Plankton is the sweet one. He didn't even rewrite it. He just swapped the names.

And then there's version three. Karen. The only person in this entire episode with no reason to lie. Also, very clearly, the only one who can't stand either of them.
```

### 07 · Karen's version, part one
```
So, according to Karen.

Krabs and Plankton are best friends from basically day one. They're both outcasts. Krabs is broke, Plankton's a nerd, and the other kids pick on both of them.

There's a moment at a carnival where little Krabs finds a penny on the ground. One penny. And Eugene Krabs... the same man who would one day sell SpongeBob's soul to a ghost for sixty-two cents... spends it on a balloon. For Plankton.

Remember the balloon. It comes back.
```

### 08 · Karen's version, part two
```
So the two of them save up and go to Stinky's Burgers, this disgusting restaurant at the dump. And the owner won't even serve them, because apparently they're bad for business.

Then the health department shuts Stinky's down, and the boys go, cool, we'll open our own. At the dump. They call it Plabs Burgers. Plankton, plus Krabs. Plabs.

Nobody comes. For ages. Until one day, an old man named Jenkins walks in, takes one bite... and collapses. The burger poisoned him.
```

### 09 · The fight that made the Krabby Patty
```
And instead of helping the man, they immediately start blaming each other. It's your recipe. No, it's your recipe. They grab the paper and pull until it rips straight down the middle.

During the fight, they knock over a shelf. Ingredients go everywhere. Right into the patty mix. And that accident... that random pile of stuff that fell off a shelf while two kids were wrestling... is the Krabby Patty.

Krabs is the one left holding it. So Krabs keeps it.

Plankton storms off, starts his own place, makes something called a Chum Burger, and nobody wants it. And that's the day he swears he'll steal the formula.
```

### 10 · He was in the room
```
Now, to be fair, the show has contradicted this. Another episode calls the formula an old Krabs family recipe. I'm choosing to ignore that, because this version is better, and more upsetting.

So let's say it plainly. The most famous secret recipe in cartoon history was invented by two kids. By accident. While they were breaking up. And one of them walked away with the whole thing.

Which means that every single time Plankton breaks into the Krusty Krab, he's technically trying to get back something he was in the room for.
```

### 11 · Chapter: The losing streak
```
So. How's the stealing going?

Badly. Historically badly. In his very first episode he crawls into SpongeBob's head and drives him around like a car. He's built a robot Mr. Krabs. He's tried being SpongeBob's friend. He's tried disguises, gadgets, flat-out lies, and more lasers than any restaurant owner should own... and every single one ends with him flying out the door in a very small, very sad arc.

The one time it actually works is the two thousand four movie. Plan Z. Which, of course, is Karen's plan. Plankton steals King Neptune's crown, frames Mr. Krabs for it, takes the formula, and then sticks a bucket on everybody's head so he can mind control the whole town. He even renames it. Planktopolis.

He finally wins. After all of that. And it lasts right up until SpongeBob shows up with a guitar.
```

### 12 · Even the wins are losses
```
And when he gets close without a movie budget, it's somehow worse.

In Single Cell Anniversary, Karen just goes and steals the formula. Herself. While he's busy. And Plankton is the one who ruins it.

Then there's Plankton's Regular. Plankton finally has a loyal customer. One guy. Comes in every day, eats the chum, seems to love it. Turns out Karen was paying him to do it.

Which is bleak. Now watch what Mr. Krabs does when he hears about it.
```

### 13 · Chapter: Okay, about Krabs
```
He puts chum on the Krusty Krab menu. To steal the one guy.

He runs the most successful restaurant in town, and he cannot let his former best friend have a single customer. Not even a fake one.

And once you see that, you start seeing it everywhere. In Enemy In-Law, Plankton starts dating Mr. Krabs's mom. Krabs loses his mind, which, fair. Anybody would. But his first assumption is that this has to be about the formula. Maybe it was. But it never even crosses his mind that something in Plankton's life might not be about him.
```

### 14 · One Coarse Meal
```
Then there's One Coarse Meal. Krabs finds out Plankton is terrified of whales. Krabs's daughter, Pearl, is a whale. So Krabs starts dressing up as her. Over and over. Popping up wherever Plankton goes.

And it stops being a bit. He keeps going until Plankton is in a genuinely dark place, for a kids' show. Dark enough that SpongeBob, a guy who thinks Mr. Krabs hung the moon, finally tells him he's gone too far.

This is also the man who sold SpongeBob's soul for sixty-two cents, and when Squidward called him out, his big question was whether he could've gotten more.

So yeah. Plankton's the villain. He'll tell you that himself. He's got the laugh and the lair and the one big eyebrow. But if you just watch what Krabs actually does, episode after episode... the crab has a lot to answer for.
```

### 15 · Chapter: Neither of them wants it to end
```
And here's the episode that convinced me this whole thing is a breakup. Plankton Retires. Written, by the way, by Mister Lawrence himself.

Plankton announces he's done. He's closing the Chum Bucket and moving into a retirement home. And Mr. Krabs cannot let it go. He drags SpongeBob over there and spies on Plankton from a ceiling fan, because he refuses to believe it.

And he's right. The retired Plankton is a robot. The real one is back at the Krusty Krab. But then Krabs reveals that he sent robots too. Both of them built fake versions of themselves... so they could keep an eye on each other... while keeping an eye on each other.

At that point you're basically checking your ex's Instagram from a second account.

And Plankton did finally get his own movie, on Netflix, in twenty twenty-five. Twenty-six years of losing to a crab, he finally gets a movie with his name on it... and the villain is his wife.
```

### 16 · The verdict
```
So. Who's the bad guy?

Remember how Friend or Foe ends? After all three versions, Krabs and Plankton actually make up. For about a minute, they're friends again. And then Plankton picks the formula out of Krabs's pocket, and they're right back to chasing each other. They had the chance, and both of them picked the chase.

But only one of them can end this. And it's Krabs. He's got the formula, the restaurant, the money, and the whole town on his side. He could hand Plankton half that recipe tomorrow. Plankton was there. He helped make it.

He never will. Because the day Plankton gets the formula, Plankton has no reason to come over anymore.

Remember the balloon? The first penny Eugene Krabs ever found, he spent on Plankton.

Every penny after that... he kept.
```
