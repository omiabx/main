# AI SCRIPT PATTERNS I CHECKED FOR AND REMOVED
These are the tells that make a voiceover sound machine-written. The script was written against this list and then audited line by line.

**Openers/closers:** "In a world where…", "Have you ever wondered…", "Imagine this:", "Let's dive in", "Buckle up", "Without further ado", "In conclusion", "At the end of the day", "Ultimately", "So the next time you…"

**Structures:**
- "It's not just X, it's Y" / "This isn't about X. It's about Y." (the #1 tell)
- Reflexive groups of three ("bold, brave, and brilliant")
- Every paragraph ending on a neat aphorism
- Uniform sentence length (AI rhythm runs at ~15–20 words, every line)
- "But here's the thing / here's the twist / the truth is"
- Fake suspense ("What happened next shocked everyone")
- Both-sidesing everything with no opinion

**Vocabulary:** delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, navigate, intricate, pivotal, iconic, unravel, journey, "stands as", "serves as a reminder", "a masterclass in", "it's worth noting".

**Punctuation:** em-dash overuse, colons before every reveal. (ElevenLabs also reads em dashes inconsistently.)

**What the script does instead (written to be spoken):**
- Contractions, fragments, and self-corrections ("Not a little bit. Like, the whole thing.")
- A real opinion, and it commits to a verdict
- Specific details from the show (Plabs, Old Man Jenkins, 62 cents) instead of vague summary
- Jokes that come from facts, not from wacky adjectives
- One planted open loop (the penny) paid off in the last line, plus one callback (Plabs)
- Rhythm variety: one-word sentences next to long rambling ones, like a person talking
- One comment prompt, near the end, framed as an actual question, never at the start
