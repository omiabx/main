# EDIT PLAN: "Mr. Krabs Lied To You About Plankton"

## Why this won't look like an AI channel
AI SpongeBob videos all follow one template: a deep "documentary" narrator, stock music, slow Ken Burns pans over AI stills, and a fact list read top to bottom. We do the opposite.

1. **A recurring format device.** The video is a trial. Every piece of evidence gets an **EXHIBIT stamp** (A, B, C…) with a paper-thud SFX. A **"KRABS: CHARGES" counter** sits in the corner during vo_07 and ticks up with a cash-register *ka-chunk*. Viewers stay to see the counter climb.
2. **No music, so silence becomes a tool.** Right before each reveal we cut *all* audio for 6–10 frames, then hit. Music-bed videos can't use silence, so ours will feel different from the first ten seconds.
3. **The show talks back.** The VO stops and an actual line from the episode answers the narrator ("…and he's lying." → hard cut to Krabs' face). Clips act as punchlines, not wallpaper.
4. **Open loops on screen.** When the penny is mentioned, a small penny icon pins to the top-right corner and stays there until the ending pays it off.
5. **A scale gag.** Plankton's intro uses a fake microscope UI (crosshair, "MAGNIFICATION 400x") that zooms in on an empty-looking frame until he appears.
6. **Pacing rule:** the image changes every 2–4 s. Never hold a static frame longer than 5 s without a punch-in, a stamp or a cut.

Canvas 1920×1080, 30 fps. Kinetic captions: big 2–4 word chunks, white with a black stroke, keyword in yellow.
