### COLD OPEN

[LABEL: PATTY HYPE · 2001]

Mr. Krabs is standing in his empty restaurant. Business is dying and he's out of ideas, so he does what a lot of people do when the money runs out. He throws his claws up at the ceiling and prays. (V01)

[CLIP: Patty Hype · Mr. Krabs] "Great Neptune in heaven! I need an idea!" (V01)

And it works. A beam of light comes down, and SpongeBob floats in on it dressed as an angel, playing a harp. (V02)

[CLIP: Patty Hype · SpongeBob, singing] "Mr. Krabs, I have an idea!" (V02)

Now, SpongeBob had been standing across the room saying that exact sentence three times in a row. Krabs only heard it once it came from above. (V03)

So I went looking. I pulled every transcript on the SpongeBob wiki, which is more than eleven hundred of them once you count the spin-offs and the games, and I searched them for anything holy. (V04) What I found is a town with a god who lives above the clouds, a ghost who comes for you when you die, a place where bad fish go, and a holiday that had to be imported from Texas. What it doesn't have is a church. Apart from one, which we'll get to at the end, and which is made of plastic.

### PART 1: THE GOD UPSTAIRS

[LABEL: NEPTUNE'S SPATULA · 2000]

The god of Bikini Bottom is King Neptune, and the show doesn't hide it. His first big appearance is Neptune's Spatula, from two thousand. SpongeBob is at the Fry Cook Museum and accidentally pulls a golden spatula out of a barrel of ancient grease, which is the sea's version of the sword in the stone. Clouds roll in across the ceiling, and Neptune shows up to meet his new fry cook. (V05) Then he sees who it is.

[CLIP: Neptune's Spatula · King Neptune] "Puny, insignificant, a commoner. Therefore, you could never be fry cook to a god, and this is why it is funny!" (V06)

He sets up a cooking contest, and to show what's at stake, he pulls back the clouds. The first time, he accidentally opens them on a live-action man taking a shower, which is genuinely in the episode. (V07) Second try:

[CLIP: Neptune's Spatula · King Neptune] "Now behold, my beloved home of Atlantis, a prize worthy of Apollo. You will reside here in this glorious place, cook only for me, and be a god." (V08)

So there's a heaven, it's up in the clouds, and the job opening is fry cook. SpongeBob wins the contest, and then says he'd rather stay home with his friends. Neptune takes that the way gods tend to. (V09)

[CLIP: Neptune's Spatula · King Neptune] "You're going to be a god and like it!" (V09)

[LABEL: THE SPONGEBOB SQUAREPANTS MOVIE · 2004]

Four years later, the movie shows us how he runs things. In his throne room, a man in chains confesses to touching the royal crown. It turns out he's the royal crown polisher, which Neptune treats as a mitigating factor. (V10)

[CLIP: The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie · King Neptune] "Well, then I guess I can't execute you. Twenty years in the dungeon it is." (V10)

Later he walks into a signpost outside the Krusty Krab. (V11)

[CLIP: The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie · King Neptune] "Have this pole executed at once." (V11)

[LABEL: THE CLASH OF TRITON · 2010]

And then there's his family. In The Clash of Triton, from twenty ten, Neptune's son Triton is fascinated by mortals and has been studying chemistry. One day he shows his father a cure for every mortal disease. Neptune knocks it out of his hand. (V12)

[CLIP: The Clash of Triton · King Neptune] "We are gods! We don't have diseases, nor do we care whether or not the mortals contract them!" (V12)

Then he locks his own son in a magic cage, hoists it up a tree on an island, and tells him to reflect on it for ten thousand years. (V13) That's the god this town prays to. And it really does pray to him.

### PART 2: THE FAITHFUL

[CARD: PART 2 · THE FAITHFUL]

In Bikini Bottom, every "thank God" becomes "thank Neptune." Across the transcripts, spin-offs and games included, "thank Neptune" turns up forty-seven times. (V14)

[LABEL: MERMAID MAN AND BARNACLE BOY II · 2000]
[CLIP: Mermaid Man and Barnacle Boy II · Mermaid Man] "Oh, my Neptune! He's been horribly disfigured!" (V15)
[LABEL: DOING TIME · 2002]
[CLIP: Doing Time · Mrs. Puff] "Alive! I'm alive! Oh, thank Neptune." (V16)

Mostly it's just a reflex, the way "oh my god" is for a lot of people. Twenty-two years later, Mrs. Puff is still calling on him, this time while she spins across a sheet of ice with SpongeBob at the wheel. (V18)

[LABEL: STUDENT DRIVER SURVIVOR · 2024]
[CLIP: Student Driver Survivor · Mrs. Puff] "Turn into the slide! Put off the gas! ___ Neptune!" (V18: NEEDS YOUR EAR, see report)

But go back to Patty Hype for a second, because I skipped the best part. Krabs prays to Neptune, the angel shows up, and the second the song is over, Krabs grabs him. (V20)

[CLIP: Patty Hype · Mr. Krabs] "What in the name of money are you waiting for, boy? Tell me!" (V20)

He prays in the name of Neptune and swears in the name of money, about five seconds apart. I know which one I'd bet he means.

### PART 3: THE AFTERLIFE

[CARD: PART 3 · THE AFTERLIFE]

If you have a god, you need an answer for what happens when you die, and Bikini Bottom's answer is in a season three episode called Born Again Krabs. (V21) "Born again" is a phrase from evangelical Christianity, sitting right there in the title of a kids' cartoon.

[LABEL: BORN AGAIN KRABS · 2002]

Krabs eats a Krabby Patty so old that it has to be kept in a cage, because it growls. He ends up in hospital. His doctor reads his chart, says "don't touch me," and runs out of the room screaming. Then the room goes green, and in floats the Flying Dutchman, carrying a sack with the word "SOULS" written on it. (V21, V22, V23)

[CLIP: Born Again Krabs · Flying Dutchman] "Eugene Krabs! Your time has come!" (V23)

Krabs tries pretending to be someone called Harold Flower, which buys him about ten seconds. (V24) Then the Dutchman explains where they're headed.

[CLIP: Born Again Krabs · Flying Dutchman] "I'm here to escort you to the resting place of all bad undersea folk. Davy Jones' locker!" (V25)

Krabs says he isn't a bad crab, so the Dutchman reads him the charge.

[CLIP: Born Again Krabs · Flying Dutchman] "Ah, but you were cheap, and being cheap is a terrible thing." (V26)

So this place has judgment, and the sin it cares about is being cheap. Davy Jones' locker turns out to be an actual gym locker stuffed with Davy Jones' socks, which is a great joke and a horrible way to spend eternity. (V27) Krabs curls up on the floor, sobs, begs for another chance, and gets one, on one condition. Always be generous, never cheap. (V28)

He comes back a changed crab, handing out free toys and free refills. Then a credit card bill for ten thousand dollars arrives, the register is empty, and the old Krabs comes right back. Before long he's threatening to pull a customer's arm off over a penny. (V29)

The Dutchman returns. SpongeBob stands up for his boss and bets his own soul that Krabs would never sell him out. So the Dutchman offers Krabs a choice between SpongeBob and all the money in his pocket. (V30)

[CLIP: Born Again Krabs · Mr. Krabs / Flying Dutchman] "That depends. How much money are we talking about?" / "62 cents." / "I'll take the money." (V31)

Sixty-two cents. SpongeBob gets dragged off to the locker, and the only reason he comes back is that the Dutchman can't stand listening to him talk. (V32)

[LABEL: JUST ONE BITE · 2001]

Squidward gets his own glimpse of the afterlife in Just One Bite, also from season three. SpongeBob tells him a Krabby Patty is good for his soul, and the background behind SpongeBob turns into heaven, with wings and a halo. (V33)

[CLIP: Just One Bite · Squidward] "Oh, puh-lease. I have no soul." (V34)

And the background behind Squidward turns into a cave full of live-action fire. When it vanishes, he looks honestly startled, and then he walks away. (V34)

### PART 4: HOLIDAYS WITHOUT A CHURCH

[CARD: PART 4 · HOLIDAYS WITHOUT A CHURCH]

Every religion has a calendar, and the big date in Bikini Bottom is Christmas. The show even tells us when it arrived. In Christmas Who?, from two thousand, Patchy the Pirate says this. (V35)

[LABEL: CHRISTMAS WHO? · 2000]
[CLIP: Christmas Who? · Patchy the Pirate] "But you know they didn't always celebrate Christmas in Bikini Bottom." (V35)

SpongeBob learns about it from Sandy, the squirrel from Texas, who's putting lights on her tree. He thinks the tree is on fire and runs in with a bucket of water. (V36)

[CLIP: Christmas Who? · Sandy / SpongeBob / Sandy] "Ain't you never seen a Christmas tree before?" / "Christmas who?" / "What? Y'all never heard of Christmas?" (V37)

Someone arrives from very far away with a holiday the locals have never heard of, and by the next day SpongeBob is standing on a table at the Krusty Krab, spreading the word. Historically, the person in Sandy's role is called a missionary. The version she brings is toy-making elves and a man called Santa Claus. (V38) Patrick's take is that Santa sounds like a genie, and Krabs treats his letter to Santa as a financial instrument. (V39)

[CLIP: Christmas Who? · Mr. Krabs] "This thing is as good as a blank check direct from the First National Bank of Santa Claus." (V39)

Nobody in the episode mentions a manger. (V40)

[LABEL: GONE · 2008]

The town's other holiday is newer and a lot darker. In Gone, from season six, SpongeBob wakes up and everyone in Bikini Bottom has vanished. He spends weeks alone. He starts doing impressions of his friends, and at one point he asks his boat to help him cook. (V41) When everybody finally comes back on a bus, Krabs explains that it was National No SpongeBob Day, and walks him through the celebrations. (V42)

[CLIP: Gone · Mr. Krabs] "First we build a giant wooden effigy o' you, then we burn it to the ground! Woo-hoo! Burn, baby! And dance on the ashes like there was no tomorrow!" (V43)

The biggest organized ritual we ever see in this town is everybody leaving home together to burn a wooden SpongeBob. And SpongeBob is touched.

[CLIP: Gone · SpongeBob] "Wow! A whole day dedicated to me!" (V44)

### PART 5: THE ONLY CHURCH IN BIKINI BOTTOM

[CARD: PART 5 · THE ONLY CHURCH IN BIKINI BOTTOM]

I said there's no church. Search the transcripts for the word and it comes up three times. Twice it's a note that a church bell sound effect plays under a joke. The third time, Patrick is getting Perch Perkins' name wrong and calls him Church Curtains. (V45) And the word "amen" doesn't appear in a single episode transcript. (V46)

[LABEL: CLUB SPONGEBOB · 2002]

There is exactly one episode where people sit down and worship something, and it's Club SpongeBob. SpongeBob and Patrick have a clubhouse on top of a tall kelp stalk, Squidward climbs in to prove a point, and the stalk catapults all three of them into the Kelp Forest. They're lost. SpongeBob pulls out a pull-string toy, the Magic Conch Shell, and Squidward calls it a stupid toy. (V47)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · SpongeBob] "Squidward, we must never question the wisdom of the Magic Conch!" (V48)

They ask the shell how to get out of the forest, and it says "Nothing." So they sit down and do nothing. Squidward storms off to save himself, gets hopelessly lost, and ends up building a camp right next to them, where he cooks a bug for dinner and gloats. (V49)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · Squidward] "As if the answers to all your problems will fall right out of the sky! Ha ha ha! Fall right out of the sky!" (V49)

At that exact moment, a plane from Picnic Supply Incorporated, which happens to be crashing, dumps its entire cargo on the two believers. (V50)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · pilot] "Dude, we're falling right out of the sky! We gotta drop the load!" (V50)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · SpongeBob & Patrick] "Praise the Magic Conch!" (V51)

Squidward wanders over to the banquet, and he's accused of heresy almost immediately. (V52)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · SpongeBob] "Squidward! Are you questioning the authority of the Magic Conch? The conch is the one who gave us this banquet. This copyrighted conch is the cornerstone of our organization." (V52)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · Patrick, whispering] "Maybe he's not a brother." (V53)

So Squidward tries to play along and asks the shell if he can eat. The shell says no. Patrick asks on Squidward's behalf, and the shell says no again. Then Patrick asks if he can have the sandwich himself, and the shell says yes. (V54)

Then a park ranger shows up to rescue them, and he's holding a Magic Conch of his own. (V55)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · Ranger] "My conch told me to come save you guys!" (V55)

So now it's a congregation. The ranger asks his conch what they should do now. It says nothing. All three of them sit down on the ground, and Squidward, the one who walked into the woods to prove them wrong, kneels down next to them. (V56)

[CLIP: Club SpongeBob · Squidward] "All hail the Magic Conch!" (V56)
