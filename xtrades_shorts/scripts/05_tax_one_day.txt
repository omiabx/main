If you sell a winning stock within a year, the IRS taxes that profit like your paycheck, at rates up to 37%.
Hold it for more than a year, and the federal rate drops to 0%, 15% or 20%.
In 2026, a single person pays 0% on those long-term gains with taxable income up to $49,450.
Say you are in the 22% tax bracket and you make a $10,000 profit.
If you sell too early, you owe about $2,200 in federal tax.
If you hold past one year, you owe about $1,500, so you keep about $700 more.
The trap is how the days are counted, because the clock starts the day after you buy.
If you bought on March 3, selling on March 3 next year is still short-term, so you would need to wait until March 4.
Your state may also charge its own tax on top.
