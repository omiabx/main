On June 4, 2026, the rule that made you keep $25,000 in your account to day trade often was removed.
Day trading means you buy and sell the same stock on the same day.
Under the old rule, four day trades in five business days got you labeled a pattern day trader, and then you needed $25,000 to keep going.
The new rule checks how much risk you take during the day instead of counting your trades.
You still need $2,000 to borrow from your broker in a margin account.
With a cash account, you can start with any amount, but money from a sale takes one business day to settle.
Say you buy and sell a stock with $500 before lunch.
If you buy again with that money and sell before it settles, your broker can mark it as a good faith violation.
The trap is thinking every app has switched already, because brokers have until October 20, 2027 to make the change.
