## 01 How much money do you need to day trade?
- $25K / pattern day trader removed, SEC approval Apr 14 2026, effective Jun 4 2026, phase-in to Oct 20 2027: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10 ; https://www.acaglobal.com/industry-insights/finra-ends-the-pattern-day-trader-rule/ ; https://tradezero.com/en-us/blog/the-usd25-000-day-trading-minimum-is-gone-here-s-what-it-means-for-you
- Old test: 4 day trades in 5 business days: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pattern_day_trader
- New rule = intraday margin (risk), not trade counting: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10
- $2,000 margin minimum (Rule 4210(b)(4)) still applies: https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/pattern-day-trader-rule-eliminated-2026
- T+1 settlement (since May 28 2024): https://sec.gov/oiea/investor-alerts-and-bulletins/new-t1-settlement-cycle-what-investors-need-know-investor

## 02 Can you really day trade for a living?
- 97% of those persisting >300 days lost money; no learning; HFT competition: https://ideas.repec.org/p/fgv/eesptd/525.html
- Authors (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti), 2013-2015 starters, 1,551 persisted, 17 (1.1%) beat min wage (~US$16/day): https://quantpedia.com/retail-day-trading-is-an-uphill-battle/ ; https://econpapers.repec.org/paper/spawpaper/2019wpecon47.htm

- Execution at or better than NBBO: https://www.sec.gov/comments/s7-31-22/s73122-20162940-332883.pdf ; https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IF12332.html

## 04 How to read a candlestick
- Body = open to close, green/red, wicks = high/low, hollow candle convention: https://www.ig.com/uk/learn/trading/candlestick-basics-how-to-read-a-candlestick-chart ; https://www.forex.com/en-uk/news-and-analysis/how-to-read-candlestick-charts/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candlestick_chart
- Long wick = price moved far then reversed before close: https://www.ig.com/uk/learn/trading/candlestick-basics-how-to-read-a-candlestick-chart
- Example candle ($100/$106/$97/$104) is illustrative, labeled as an example on screen.

## 05 Why holding one extra day can cut your tax
- Short-term (<=1 yr) taxed as ordinary income; long-term 0/15/20%: https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409
- 2026 brackets (top 37%, 22% bracket) and 2026 0% LTCG threshold $49,450 single: https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/federal/2026-tax-brackets/ ; https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/irs-updates-capital-gains-tax-thresholds ; Rev. Proc. 2025-32 https://www.eitc.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf
- Holding period starts the day after the trade date; sell date counts; exactly one year = short-term: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550 ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.1223-1
- Example math: $10,000 x 22% = $2,200; x 15% = $1,500 (a 22%-bracket single filer is in the 15% LTCG band, since that band starts at $49,451).
