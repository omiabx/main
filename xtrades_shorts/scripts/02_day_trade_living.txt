In one study of day traders in Brazil, 97% of the people who kept going for more than 300 days lost money.
Three economists tracked everyone who started day trading futures there between 2013 and 2015.
Only 1,551 people lasted 300 days or more.
Out of that group, just 17 people earned more than Brazil's minimum wage, which was about $16 a day.
The researchers also found that people did not get better with practice.
Part of the reason is that you are trading against big firms with much faster computers.
This study covers one country and one market, so it is not the final word on trading.
It does show how rare it is to pay your bills with day trading alone.
The trap is quitting your job after one good month, because one month tells you almost nothing.
