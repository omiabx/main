## VOICEOVER CHUNKS

### CHUNK 1: Cold open
```
This sign is on the road out of Bikini Bottom. Population... five hundred and thirty-eight.

In one episode, SpongeBob decides the town doesn't need him anymore. He packs a bag, walks out, stops at this sign... and crosses out the eight.

Five hundred and thirty-seven. He updated the census on his way out of town, with a marker.

Which would be totally fine, except seven years earlier, forty-six thousand, eight hundred and fifty-three people stood in line for a burger.

That's eighty-seven Bikini Bottoms standing in one line for a pink hamburger.

So somebody's lying. And I spent way too long going through this show, pausing crowd shots and counting fish one by one, to find out who.

I got an answer. It's not the one on the sign. And the last part of it is a lot darker than a population count has any right to be.
```

### CHUNK 2: The sign
```
Okay, let's start by actually believing the sign. Five hundred and thirty-eight people is not a town. That's a village. That's a place where everybody knows whose snail pooped on whose lawn.

And if you only watch the main cast, it kind of holds up. SpongeBob, Patrick, Squidward, Mr. Krabs, Sandy, Plankton, Karen, Mrs. Puff, Pearl. Gary, I guess. He doesn't pay taxes, but he's in.

Add Larry, Mermaid Man, Barnacle Boy, the Flying Dutchman, and the guy who only ever yells "my leg"... and we're at about fifteen. Five hundred and twenty-three to go. Very doable.

The problem is, this town keeps doing things that five hundred people can't do.
```

### CHUNK 3: The line
```
Patty Hype, season two. SpongeBob invents the Pretty Patty, a Krabby Patty that comes in colors, and Mr. Krabs basically laughs him out of the building.

So SpongeBob sets up his own little stand. And the next time Mr. Krabs walks by... this is the line.

Forty-six thousand, eight hundred and fifty-three. And that's not SpongeBob going "a bajillion people!" Somebody actually counted.

If the sign is right, every single person in Bikini Bottom would have to get in that line eighty-seven times. Which, for a purple burger... okay, maybe.

Or there's a much bigger town hiding behind that sign.
```

### CHUNK 4: The witness
```
And if anyone would know, it's Mrs. Puff. Nobody alive has spent more time staring at Bikini Bottom traffic in pure terror.

Here's what she thinks happens if SpongeBob ever gets his license.

Millions. Now, she's panicking, she's a pufferfish, she's fully inflated, I'm not treating that like an official government document. But she drives those roads every day. And she didn't say hundreds.

So now we've got three numbers. A village, a city, and something closer to a country. Time to stop listening to people and start counting them.
```

### CHUNK 5: The stadium
```
Band Geeks. Squidward brags that he's leading a band at the Bubble Bowl. Which, by the way, is a stadium. Bikini Bottom has a stadium.

So I paused on the stands and counted every fish I could see. One by one.

[COUNT]. And that's one shot. The stands keep going off-screen in every direction.

Five hundred and thirty-eight people couldn't fill one section of this place.
```

### CHUNK 6: Same guy
```
But while I was counting, something started bugging me.

This guy. I already counted this guy. He's over here. And here. And he's also over here, wearing a different shirt.

The animators don't draw thousands of different fish. They've got a set of background designs, and they reuse them in every crowd. The crew even numbers them instead of naming them.

So there's another theory. Maybe Bikini Bottom really is tiny. Maybe it's like forty fish, and they're just... everywhere. In the line. At the stadium. At the Krusty Krab. Breaking their leg.

Which would explain the Pretty Patty line. It's the same forty guys going around to the back.
```

### CHUNK 7: The stuff
```
Except you can't run a town on forty guys. Look at what Bikini Bottom actually has.

A hospital. A police force. A stadium. A mall. An entire theme park built around a glove. A boating school. And two restaurants that have been at war over the same customers for more than twenty-five years.

A real town of five hundred people has a gas station. And one guy named Dale who owns it.

A theme park needs visitors. A stadium needs people in the seats. And Plankton has spent his whole life trying to steal one recipe. Nobody plans a twenty-five year heist over five hundred customers.
```

### CHUNK 8: The verdict
```
So here's where I landed.

The sign is real. It's just old. Somebody painted it when Bikini Bottom was a couple of streets, and the only person who's ever updated it is a sponge with a marker.

The best evidence we have is that line, because it's the only time anyone in this show actually counted. Forty-six thousand people showed up for a novelty burger. Not everyone in town drops everything for a burger. But if most of them did...

Bikini Bottom has somewhere around fifty thousand people.

Fifty thousand is a real town. That's enough for a stadium, a hospital, a glove-based theme park, and a restaurant war. It means the sign is off by about forty-nine and a half thousand people. And it means Mrs. Puff was exaggerating. Which, if you've ever seen her face when SpongeBob turns the key... fair.
```

### CHUNK 9: The other Bikini
```
There's one more population I need to tell you about, though.

Bikini Bottom sits underneath a real place. Bikini Atoll. A ring of tiny islands in the middle of the Pacific. And in nineteen forty-six, it had a population too.

One hundred and sixty-seven people.

That year, the United States Navy asked them to leave, so the islands could be used to test atomic bombs. Their leader, King Juda, agreed. He said they would go, and leave everything in the hands of God.

They were moved to another island that couldn't feed them. Then another. Some of them came back in the seventies, and had to be evacuated all over again, because the ground was still radioactive.

Today, the population of Bikini Atoll is about five. Caretakers.

SpongeBob crossed out one number on a sign, and the whole town noticed. The real Bikini lost a hundred and sixty-two, and most people have never even heard of it.

If you want to know what's actually sitting at the bottom of the real Bikini, I made a whole video about it. It's right here.
```
