### CHUNK 5: The stadium
```
Band Geeks. Squidward promises his band is going to play the Bubble Bowl. Which, by the way, is a stadium. Bikini Bottom has a stadium.

So I went to the big game, paused on the crowd, and got ready to count every single fish.

Hang on to that toxic-waste line. We're coming back to it.

### CHUNK 6: Same guy
```
But while I was counting, something started bugging me.

This guy. I already counted this guy. He's right here... and he's also right here. And this one's over here, and also over here, in a completely different outfit.

The animators don't draw thousands of different fish. They've got a set of background designs, and they reuse them in every crowd. The crew even numbers them instead of naming them.

So there's another theory. Maybe Bikini Bottom really is tiny. Maybe it's like forty fish, and they're just... everywhere. In the line. At the Krusty Krab. At the stadium, apparently disguised as humans. Breaking their leg.

Which would explain the Pretty Patty line. It's the same forty guys going around to the back. And honestly, the episode kind of admits it.
```

### CHUNK 7: The stuff
```
Except you can't run a town on forty guys. Look at what Bikini Bottom actually has.

A hospital. A police force. A stadium. A mall. An entire theme park built around a glove. A boating school. And two restaurants that have been at war over the same customers for more than twenty-five years.

A real town of five hundred people has a gas station. And one guy named Dale who owns it.

A theme park needs visitors. A stadium needs people in the seats. And Plankton has spent his whole life trying to steal one recipe. Nobody plans a twenty-five year heist over five hundred customers.
```

### CHUNK 8: The verdict
```
So here's where I landed.

The sign is real. It's just old. Somebody painted it when Bikini Bottom was a couple of streets, and the only person who's ever updated it is a sponge with a piece of chalk.

The best evidence we have is that line, because it's the only time anyone in this show actually counted. They were handing out numbers. Forty-six thousand people showed up for a novelty burger. Not everyone in town drops everything for a burger. But if most of them did...

Bikini Bottom has somewhere around fifty thousand people.

Fifty thousand is a real town. That's enough for a stadium, a hospital, a glove-based theme park, and a restaurant war. It means the sign is off by about forty-nine and a half thousand people. And it means Mrs. Puff was exaggerating. Which, if you've ever seen her face when SpongeBob turns the key... fair.
```

### CHUNK 9: The other Bikini
```
There's one more population I need to tell you about, though.

Bikini Bottom sits underneath a real place. Bikini Atoll. A ring of tiny islands in the middle of the Pacific. And in nineteen forty-six, it had a population too.

One hundred and sixty-seven people.

That year, the United States Navy asked them to leave, so the islands could be used to test atomic bombs. Their leader, King Juda, agreed. He said they would go, and leave everything in the hands of God.

They were moved to another island that couldn't feed them. Then another. Some of them came back in the seventies, and had to be evacuated all over again, because the ground was still radioactive.

Remember the Bubble Bowl? "Maybe we're near one of those toxic-waste dumps."

SpongeBob, buddy. You live under a nuclear test site.

Today, the population of Bikini Atoll is about five. Caretakers.

SpongeBob crossed out one number on a sign, and the whole town noticed. The real Bikini lost a hundred and sixty-two, and most people have never even heard of it.

If you want to know what's actually sitting at the bottom of the real Bikini, I made a whole video about it. It's right here.
