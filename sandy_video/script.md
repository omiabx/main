# Why Sandy Cheeks Lives in Bikini Bottom: Script & Voiceover Chunks

**Working title:** Why Sandy Actually Lives in Bikini Bottom (her contract just ran out)
**Alt titles:** Sandy Cheeks' Contract Expired This Year / Sandy Cheeks Is Out of Contract and Nobody Noticed
**Target length:** ~7 min. No music. SFX + original show audio only.

## ElevenLabs settings

- Model: **Eleven Multilingual v2** (most natural for long narration). Don't paste audio tags like [laughs]; they get read out on v2.
- Voice: a conversational, slightly dry male or female narrator. Avoid "trailer" or "documentary" voices.
- Stability ~40–45%, Similarity ~75%, Style ~15–20%, Speaker Boost on.
- One generation per chunk. Name files **01.mp3, 02.mp3 … 22.mp3**.
- If a name comes out wrong, regenerate just that chunk. Watch "Sue Nah-mee" (chunk 18) and "B-O-O-T-S" (chunk 17).
