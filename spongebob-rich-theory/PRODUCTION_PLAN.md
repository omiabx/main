# Production Plan: The SpongeBob RICH Theory

## Style rules (how this avoids the "AI video" look)
- **No music at all.** Silence is used on purpose: the half-second of dead air before "sixty-two cents" and "boots" does the job music usually does.
- **SFX only**, synthesized in-house so there are no license issues: cash register, coin drops, whooshes, impact hits, record scratch, receipt printer, calculator beeps, camera shutter, a "ding" on every number reveal.
- **Edits come from the show, not from stock footage.** Clips are freeze-framed, punched in, rewound, or put on a "receipt" or "bank statement" graphic. Each number gets a running **SpongeBob ledger** on screen, a receipt that keeps printing as evidence piles up: `Boots ×1`, `Market value: $0.62`, `Nickel: -$0.05` … and ends with `OWED: $470,000`.
- No stock "cinematic" B-roll, no glowing text, and no Ken Burns slideshow over AI images. Captions are punchy, two to four words at a time, with key numbers in yellow.
